London Rivers

Ductwork installation in the ceiling voids of a busy building-services package, coordinated around air handling units, pipework and cabling.

Project

Our role
Ductwork installation
Installed

Year

01 / The job

Tight voids.
Clear routes.

Coreair installed ductwork on the London Rivers project, working in the ceiling voids alongside sprinkler pipework, cable containment and cabling.

The work covered insulated circular and rectangular ductwork, branch connections and the final connections to the ceiling-suspended air handling units, with access panels and flow-direction markings fitted along the runs so the system can be commissioned and maintained.

The photographs below were taken on site during the installation.
